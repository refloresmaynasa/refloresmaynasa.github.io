# Mission

## What this site is

A personal brand hub for **Ricardo Edwin Flores Maynasa** — not just a CV link to paste into job applications, but a public professional identity that shows who Ricardo is and what he delivers.

## Who it is for

| Audience | What they need to see |
|---|---|
| **Recruiters / hiring managers** | Depth and reliability — 10+ years, enterprise scale, real outcomes |
| **Freelance / contract clients** | Range and problem-solving — polyglot engineer who thinks in business outcomes, not just code |

## What visitors should feel when they leave

> *"This person is a senior, reliable engineer — versatile across the full stack — and they think in outcomes, not just code."*

Three impressions, in priority order:

1. **Reliable** — 10+ years of sustained, enterprise-grade delivery
2. **Versatile** — Java, .NET, cloud, databases, DevOps: comfortable across the whole stack
3. **Outcome-focused** — solves problems and coordinates teams, not just writes code

## What this site is NOT

- A static PDF export
- A list of technologies without context
- A generic template that could belong to anyone

## Success criteria

A recruiter or client who lands on the site should be able to answer these three questions in under 60 seconds:

1. What does Ricardo build?
2. What has he actually delivered?
3. How do I contact him?
